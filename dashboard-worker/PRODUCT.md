# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The portfolio owner checks recent interest in his engineering work. This is a private report, not a public portfolio page. Access is limited to the owner's verified email identity.

## Product Purpose

Replace the manual D1 queries documented in ../docs/analytics.md with a simple report of CV clicks, project/GitHub links and contact activity. Make date ranges, totals and the difference between empty data and errors clear.

The owner also requested a private contact inbox and editing of homepage/contact introductions and project links. Keep these as three small sections: Statistics, Requests and Site content. Requests can be marked new, read or archived. Content changes must publish safe plain text and HTTPS destinations, with an option to restore the original copy.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise and personal. Preserve the warm paper and blue/teal identity recorded in ../docs/design.md while prioritising familiar report controls and readable numbers.

## Anti-references

The portfolio design brief rejects generic repeated feature cards, gratuitous motion and unverified claims. The analytics documentation rejects visitor-level tracking and misleading conversion funnels. The dashboard must not fabricate sample traffic or present clicks as completed downloads.

## Design Principles

- Report the aggregate data already collected; do not expand tracking.
- Keep SQL and infrastructure controls out of the owner's reading flow.
- Use explicit labels, plain language and transparent count definitions.
- Authentication and availability failures must not expose stored metrics.
- Keep the deployed report within the existing free Cloudflare account.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain the portfolio's keyboard focus and responsive accessibility practices. Controls require labels, charts need text alternatives, errors need readable recovery, and layout must work at 390px and enlarged text. No decorative motion is needed.
