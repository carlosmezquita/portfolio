# Public privacy page

The privacy page is a reading and preference-management surface within the public
portfolio. It uses the shared warm paper, ink, Instrument Serif display type and
Instrument Sans reading text. Keep the privacy policy identifiable in the title;
do not turn the page into a marketing statement or add the homepage's animation.

## Reading order and behavior

- The opening explains the page's purpose, offers a direct analytics-control link
  and summarizes browsing, enquiries and choice. The summary does not replace the
  detailed policy.
- A section index is sticky beside the reading column on desktop and appears in
  normal flow on phones. All policy sections remain expanded and readable without
  JavaScript; no accordion hides important disclosures.
- The first section contains the existing analytics controls, live setting text
  and browser-scope explanation. Both actions have equal visual weight and visible
  keyboard focus. `privacy.js` remains the existing preference implementation;
  GPC/DNT still override the Enable action, and storage failures remain explicit.
- Contact enquiries, custom statistics, Cloudflare processing and retention are
  separate sections. Distinguish custom aggregate counters from Cloudflare's
  performance beacon and transient network processing. Distinguish the private
  inbox's cleanup from delivered email copies; do not imply they share retention.
- Keep the operator, public hello alias, contact form and complaint route easy to
  find. Do not expose the personal inbox or promise blanket anonymity, EU-only
  processing, automatic deletion of email copies or legal certification.

The layout changes no analytics collection or retention setting. The privacy page
loads neither analytics collector. Preserve that property when adding features.
Print styles remove navigation and choice buttons while retaining the policy text.

## Research

The ICO's [drafting guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/how-should-we-draft-our-privacy-information/)
and [layered-notice guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-methods-can-we-use-to-provide-privacy-information/)
informed the short overview, descriptive headings, full visible detail and nearby
controls. Research reviewed 23 October 2026. This documents presentation choices;
it is not a legal assessment of the site's processing or lawful basis.

## Review checks

Check the index destinations, heading order, no-JavaScript readability, live status,
off/on actions, privacy-signal precedence, blocked storage and keyboard focus.
Inspect 1440/1024/760/390/320px layouts, readable line lengths and long-link wrapping.
Do not claim browser zoom or physical-device checks unless actually performed.
Run the existing traffic/performance privacy tests and public-contact check.
