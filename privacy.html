01 / Your choice

Your analytics choice

Optional analytics measures use of this website, not advertising or profiling. Continuing to browse is not treated as consent. Disabling analytics does not prevent browsing or using the contact form.

Current setting

Enable JavaScript to read or change your browser preference, or use Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

This choice applies to this browser and site address.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track disable optional collection, even if you select “Enable”. Saving an opt-out writes one local browser preference solely to remember your choice. It contains no identifier and is never transmitted. Clearing site data removes it. The www address and other browsers have separate preferences.

The choice prevents both collectors from loading on subsequent portfolio visits. Custom measurements also check it before each send. Reload or close other portfolio tabs to stop performance measurement loaded before your choice. Reports already sent cannot be recalled.

02 / Contact messages

When you get in touch

The contact form processes the name, reply address and message you submit so I can read and respond to your enquiry. Cloudflare Turnstile helps prevent spam and abuse.

Accepted enquiries are delivered by email and stored in the website’s private inbox for up to 90 days. Email copies are handled separately. Contact handling and the private inbox are separate from optional analytics; turning analytics off does not stop your message from being delivered.

03 / Site statistics

How site use is measured

The custom collector keeps daily totals in separate breakdowns, with no stored link between them. It measures:

Traffic
Page views, broad traffic-source categories, approximate country, browser family, device type and viewport-width bands.
Engagement
Scroll milestones, visible-tab time milestones and page-load time bands.
Actions
CV and project link clicks, contact-form starts and accepted enquiries.

What the custom collector leaves out

No cookies, visitor or session identifiers, fingerprint hashes, session recordings or analytics storage in your browser. It sends only fixed page paths and predefined categories: no query strings, URL fragments, raw referrers, form contents or precise screen sizes.

Reloads can count again. These statistics cannot identify unique visitors. Counters are not joined to contact messages, sold or used to follow visitors across websites.

04 / Cloudflare services

Performance and delivery

Performance reports

Cloudflare Web Analytics complements the custom counters with page-performance measurements, including Core Web Vitals and navigation timings. Its cookie-free beacon also reports page/referral information and an ephemeral page-load identifier; it does not create a persistent visitor profile.

It loads only when the same browser preference permits analytics. Cloudflare’s processing and retention are separate from the custom database’s 90-day cleanup. This privacy page loads neither analytics collector.

Read Cloudflare’s performance-data documentation ↗

Network information and abuse prevention

Cloudflare processes IP addresses and other network information to deliver requests, estimate country and prevent abuse. The application uses the IP transiently for rate limiting. It does not store IP addresses, the full User-Agent header or raw events in analytics tables. Application request logging is disabled for the custom collector.

Cloudflare infrastructure processing is separate from those aggregate tables and is covered by its privacy policy and data processing addendum. This notice does not claim that all infrastructure processing takes place exclusively in the EU.

05 / Storage & retention

What is kept, and for how long

The custom collector and private dashboard run on Cloudflare Workers and D1. The dashboard is restricted to the portfolio owner.

Aggregate counters
90 UTC calendar days, then deleted by a daily cleanup.
Private contact inbox
Enquiries are kept for up to 90 days, then removed by a daily cleanup.
Email copies
Handled separately from the website’s private inbox; its 90-day cleanup does not delete delivered emails.
Browser preference
Your opt-out remains in this browser until you enable analytics or clear the site’s stored data.
