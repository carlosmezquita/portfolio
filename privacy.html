Privacy Policy.

This website uses aggregate statistics to understand which pages and links are useful and to improve the site. The purpose is site measurement, not advertising or profiling.

This notice explains the measurement and your choices. Continuing to browse is not treated as consent. You can disable optional analytics below and continue using the portfolio normally.

What is measured

The optional collector counts page views, broad traffic-source categories, approximate country, browser family, device type and viewport-width bands. It also counts scroll milestones, visible-tab time milestones, page-load time bands, CV and project link clicks, contact-form starts and accepted enquiries. These are daily totals in separate breakdowns, with no stored link between them.

The collector uses no cookies, visitor or session identifiers, fingerprint hashes, session recordings or analytics storage in your browser. It sends only fixed page paths and predefined categories. It does not send query strings, URL fragments, raw referrers, form contents or precise screen sizes. Reloads can count again; these statistics cannot identify unique visitors.

Your choice

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track disable optional collection. You can also save a preference below. Saving an opt-out writes one local browser preference solely to remember your choice; it contains no identifier and is never transmitted. The choice applies separately to each browser and site origin, including the www address. Clearing site data removes it.

The choice prevents both collectors from loading on subsequent portfolio visits. Custom measurements also check the choice before each send. If you have other portfolio tabs already open, reload or close them to stop performance measurement that was loaded before your choice. Reports already sent cannot be recalled.

Enable JavaScript to read or change your browser preference, or use Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

Processing and retention

The custom collector and private dashboard run on Cloudflare Workers and D1. Daily aggregate counters are retained for 90 UTC calendar days and deleted by a daily cleanup. The dashboard is restricted to the portfolio owner. Counters are not joined to contact messages, sold or used to follow visitors across websites.

Cloudflare performance reports

Cloudflare Web Analytics complements the custom counters with page-performance measurements, including Core Web Vitals and navigation timings. Its cookie-free beacon also reports page/referral information and an ephemeral page-load identifier; it does not create a persistent visitor profile. It loads only when the same browser preference permits analytics. The privacy page itself loads neither collector. Cloudflare's processing and retention are separate from the custom database's 90-day cleanup. See Cloudflare's performance-data and privacy documentation.

Cloudflare processes IP addresses and other network information to deliver requests, estimate country and prevent abuse. The application uses the IP transiently for rate limiting and does not store it, the full User-Agent header or raw events in analytics tables. Application request logging is disabled for this collector. Cloudflare infrastructure processing is separate from these aggregate tables and is covered by its privacy policy and data processing addendum. This notice does not claim that all infrastructure processing takes place exclusively in the EU.

Contact and your rights

This website, carlosmezquita.dev, is operated by Carlos Mezquita, who is responsible for the personal information submitted through its contact form.

The intended legal basis for necessary site measurement is the legitimate interest in administering and improving this website. You may object using the controls above, or contact the website operator through the contact form about privacy, access, erasure or other applicable rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Aggregate statistics contain no visitor identifier to look up a particular person's history.

The contact form processes the name, reply address and message you choose to submit, and uses Cloudflare Turnstile for abuse prevention. Contact enquiries are stored in the website's private inbox for up to 90 days, then removed by a daily cleanup. Copies delivered by email are handled separately. Contact handling and the private inbox are separate from optional analytics. Disabling analytics does not prevent using the contact form or browsing the website. This page itself does not load the analytics collector.
